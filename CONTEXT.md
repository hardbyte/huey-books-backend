# Huey Books

## Language

**Organisation**: The administrative group that manages one or more libraries, such as a school or a public-library service.

**Education unit**: A school or independently administered division with its own student and class privacy boundary. It is distinct from both its organisation and the libraries serving it.

**Billing account**: The financial ownership boundary for an organisation or family payer, separate from a library's inventory and reader-service coverage.

**Library coverage**: The libraries to which a subscription supplies reader service; distinct from subscription ownership and organisation management entitlements.

**Organisation subscription**: A subscription owned by an organisation that can qualify it for paid management features. It does not itself activate reader access at every library.
_Avoid_: Billing library, sponsoring library.

**Feature entitlement**: An organisation's eligibility to use a paid feature. It is separate from a staff member's permission to perform an action.

**Library**: A distinct borrowing and recommendation context with its own collections. It can be a school division or public-library branch and need not correspond to a separate building.
_Avoid_: School when referring to public libraries, collection when referring to the managing context.

**Collection**: An independently maintained inventory of holdings belonging to a library or an individual reader.

**Default collection**: The library inventory selected by legacy integrations that do not specify a collection. It does not define the full library-wide recommendation scope.

**Library UUID**: The stable public identifier for a library; existing school UUIDs remain valid library identifiers during the terminology transition.

**Legacy school UUID**: The public identifier historically used for the combined school/library record, retained as the library's public identity.
_Avoid_: Wriveted identifier, school ID when referring to a public UUID.

**Legacy school ID**: The internal integer identifier for the combined school/library record, distinct from its public UUID and from an education-unit identity.
